Research

Last updated: 2026-09-25

My research is focussed on cryptanalysis of post-quantum lattice-based cryptography. I particularly find the lattice isomorphism problem interesting due to its mathematical elegance, it is typically my focus for research, and the problems that stem from it e.g. quaternion principal ideal problem. I find the quaternions and octonions also particularly interesting; if you would like to discuss or have research ideas in this area, please reach out! I have also been reading literature about well-rounded and nearly-orthogonal lattices; such geometric structure is something that intrigues me.

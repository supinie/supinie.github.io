About Me

I am currently a PhD student studying lattice-based cryptography, in Prof. Cong Ling's research group under Communications and Signals Processing, Electrical and Electronic Engineering department, Imperial College London. I primarily study lattice reduction for structured and algebraic lattices. This primarily takes the form of module lattices, though I have interest in geometrically structured lattices such as well-rounded or nearly-orthogonal lattices also. Beyond this, I am interested in composition algebras, namely the quaternions and octonions, and the computational algebraic problems associated with them; especially those that arise cryptographically.

Besides my research, I am interesting in software development, particularly software that provides safety and correctness guarantees at compile time and the functional programming paradigm. I am particularly fond of Rust and Nix. A list of some of my projects may be found below. I also dabble in some hardware projects, originating in an interest in keyboards.
